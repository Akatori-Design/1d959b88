Questions

How does studying work?
Tap a card to flip it. Swipe right if you knew the word, left if you didn't. Words you know come back after longer and longer gaps; words you miss come back sooner.
Why does it say “All caught up”?
Nothing is due for review yet. Tap Study anyway to practise every card now.
How do I add a word?
Tap the dictionary button at the top left, search, tap the word, then Add to cards.
How do I delete cards?
In the dictionary, swipe a card left, or press and hold to select several and tap the trash.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Only words that aren't in the built-in dictionary need the internet to look up.
