FlashCard Lite Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

In short: FlashCard Lite has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your cards and study progress stay on your device. The only thing that ever leaves your phone is a word you choose to look up online, and only so the dictionary service can send back its definition.

What stays on your device

  • The cards you add, and your study progress (which words you knew, and when each card is next due).
  • The built-in dictionary, which works offline.
  • Spoken pronunciations made with your iPhone's built-in voice.

This information is stored only in the app on your iPhone. We can't see it, and it isn't sent anywhere. It's included in your device's normal iCloud or computer backups if you use them.

What is sent over the internet

When a word isn't in the built-in dictionary and you tap Look up online, or when you open a word's definition with online definitions turned on, the app sends that word to a dictionary service to get its definition:

  • Wiktionary, run by the Wikimedia Foundation. See the Wikimedia privacy policy.
  • Oxford Dictionaries (Oxford University Press), if the app uses its definitions. See the Oxford University Press privacy policy. Recorded pronunciations are also played from Oxford's servers.

Only the word itself is sent, with no name, account, device ID or list of your cards. Like any internet request, the service can see your device's IP address. We don't receive, store or have access to these lookups.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect personal information.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share any data.

Children

FlashCard Lite doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children. The dictionary includes everyday slang, and some entries are labelled as vulgar.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual cards in the app (swipe left on a card in the dictionary, or press and hold to select several). Deleting the app removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
