# Welcoming Marin Ivezic as an Advisor

News · June 3, 2026

![Marin Ivezic](images/10-advisor.jpg)

**The founder of Applied Quantum and author of PostQuantum.com joins to guide enterprise adoption, risk education and standards work.**

Marin Ivezic is joining as an advisor. Marin founded Applied Quantum, a quantum security services and systems integration firm, and writes PostQuantum.com, one of the most widely read independent sources on quantum computing security.

Before that, Marin led cybersecurity practices at several of the largest global consultancies across Asia-Pacific, North America, Europe and the Middle East, and served as CISO and CTO for Fortune Global 500 organisations. Three decades of that work have centred on helping enterprises, governments and critical infrastructure operators prepare for complex security transformations.

## Why this matters now

Blockchains, custodians and financial institutions are starting to prepare for the next generation of cryptographic threats. They face the same migration challenge that governments and critical infrastructure have been working through — but with fewer regulatory levers and tighter timelines.

> Approaching quantum readiness as a practical engineering and institutional problem, rather than a theoretical debate.

## What Marin will focus on

- Enterprise adoption: how large organisations sequence a post-quantum migration across vendors, custody and internal systems.
- Risk education: helping boards and regulators understand which exposures are urgent and which can wait.
- Standards: connecting the digital asset industry's needs to the broader post-quantum standards conversation.

We are glad to have Marin's perspective as the industry moves from awareness to execution.
